# Cyclocross

## Belgium race registration

Use the My Cycling Vlaanderen portal to register for cyclocross races in Belgium, typically C1 and C2 races, and manage your rider details. You must be known to Cycling Vlaanderen to sign in. Foreign licence holders can create an account through the portal.

Register for an account or race with [My Cycling Vlaanderen](https://portal.cycling.vlaanderen/)
